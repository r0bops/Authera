## Scope and mode

Client routes under `/dashboard`. Operate mode.

## Audience and job

A nontechnical person delegates a bounded purchase, checks whether the agent is making progress, decides exact exceptions, and stops authorization when desired.

## Primary task

State the outcome once, review the plain-language limits, authorize it, then supervise by exception instead of repeatedly searching and booking.

## Proof and constraints

Show live agent state, the standing authorization, best real offer, pending human decisions, completed purchases, and immediate revocation. Keep deterministic gateway evidence available but subordinate. Preserve `/agent`, `/verify`, `/audit`, and `/demo` as separate actor perspectives.

## Direction

Concierge command center: attention first, followed by one active purchase brief and its live progression. The memorable moment is a single plain-language sentence connecting what the person asked for, what Aria is doing now, and exactly what happens next.
